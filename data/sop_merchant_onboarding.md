# SOP: Merchant onboarding (Kestrel Pay — FICTIONAL)

Version 3.2 · Owner: Head of Onboarding · Applies to all new merchants

## 1. Intake

The onboarding associate reviews each application for the 23 mandatory fields and the four mandatory documents. Incomplete applications are returned to the merchant with a list of missing items using template RTN-01.

## 2. Business verification

The associate confirms the registration number against the national business registry and checks that the company name, registration date and directors match the uploaded certificate. Discrepancies go to a compliance analyst.

## 3. Identity and screening

Every director and beneficial owner above 25% is verified through the identity vendor and screened against sanctions and PEP lists. Partial matches must be cleared or escalated by a compliance analyst, and the rationale recorded in the case file.

## 4. Business-model review

A compliance analyst reviews the merchant's website and stated business model against the prohibited and restricted activities policy. Restricted categories (for example crypto services, remittance, gaming) require enhanced due diligence.

## 5. Risk tiering and approval

The analyst scores the merchant on the nine-factor risk matrix. Low and medium risk merchants may be approved by a senior analyst. High-risk merchants require MLRO approval. Final approval or decline is always recorded against a named approver.

## 6. Commercial setup and go-live

Sales operations generates the contract from the standard template with the agreed pricing. After signature, the associate provisions API keys, sends the sandbox welcome email (template WEL-02) and schedules the go-live checklist call.
